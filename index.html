About me

A hands-on builder who learned by making things.

I moved from welding and fabrication into programming because I wanted work that rewarded problem-solving, creativity and constant improvement.

Long days and late nights taught me how to break down problems, test ideas, fix what fails and finish projects. My work now covers web development, Python games, mobile experiments, UI design and business software.

I also create gaming content and stream, which has pushed me to think about performance, branding, community and how people actually experience digital products—not just how the code looks.
